
🏛️ 1. Money, Banking Structure, & Interbank Liquidity
This layer monitors the absolute plumbing of the fiat credit system, balance sheet mechanics of commercial institutions, regulatory guardrails, and central bank asset positioning.

    Monetary Aggregates & Velocity:
        M1SL: M1 Money Supply (Highly liquid cash, physical currency, demand deposits).
        M2SL: M2 Money Supply (M1 + savings deposits, small-time deposits, retail money market funds).
        M2V: Velocity of M2 Money Stock (The frequency with which a dollar is spent on newly produced goods/services).
        CURRCIR: Currency in Circulation (Physical notes and coins circulating in the wild outside depository vaults).
    Federal Reserve System Balance Sheet:
        WALCL: Federal Reserve Total Assets (The primary engine of Quantitative Easing / Quantitative Tightening).
        WREBAL: Reserve Balances with Federal Reserve Banks (Total commercial bank cash held on deposit at the Fed).
        WLRRAL: Reverse Repurchase Agreements (RRP) Held by the Fed (Systemic excess liquidity parked overnight by money funds).
        TREAST: U.S. Treasury Securities Held by the Federal Reserve (Direct monetization track of national debt).
        WSHOMCB: Mortgage-Backed Securities (MBS) Held by the Federal Reserve (Real estate systemic asset support).
        RESPPLLD: Primary Credit Outstanding (Discount Window borrowing; direct indicator of acute bank liquidity emergencies).
    Commercial Bank Asset Allocations:
        TOTLL: Total Loans and Leases, All Commercial Banks (Primary aggregate metric for credit expansion).
        BUSLOANS: Commercial and Industrial (C&I) Loans, All Commercial Banks (Direct credit flow into real business operations).
        REALL: Real Estate Loans, All Commercial Banks (Aggregate real estate debt allocation).
        CONSUMER: Consumer Loans, All Commercial Banks (Auto, credit card, and traditional retail bank lines).
        INVEST: Securities in Bank Credit, All Commercial Banks (Treasuries, agencies, and sovereign bonds held inside bank capital reserves).
        CASHE: Cash Assets, All Commercial Banks (Vault cash and deposits held at other banks / the Fed).
    Deposits & Capital Liabilities:
        DPSACBM027SBOG: Deposits, All Commercial Banks (The deposit base funding bank loan assets).
        DEMDEPSL: Demand Deposits (Non-interest checking assets; fast-moving retail capital).
        SAVINGS: Savings Deposits (Slower velocity retail deposits).
        STLDEPSL: Small-Time Deposits at Commercial Banks (Retail CDs and structured high-yield cash vehicles).
        LGTDEPSL: Large-Time Deposits at Commercial Banks (Institutional scale cash deposits subject to flight risks).
    Structural Bank Reserves & Leverage Base:
        TOTRESNS: Total Reserves of Depository Institutions (The total cash baseline backing commercial credit systems).
        H6STRESNS: Required Reserves of Depository Institutions (The legal structural lock up for commercial banking institutions).
        EXCSRESNS: Excess Reserves of Depository Institutions (The structural hoard available for unexpected liquidity crises).
        BOGASSETS: Total Assets of Commercial Banks in the United States (The comprehensive macro ledger of the banking industry).
